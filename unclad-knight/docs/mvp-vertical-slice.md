# MVP Vertical Slice

## Purpose

The first slice should prove tone, movement, and narrative framing before it tries to prove scale.

Success for the slice is not "lots of systems." Success is:

- a memorable nocturnal district
- a short playable route
- one delivery framed as an oath
- one symbolic refusal that points toward the Sword of Denunciation and the Shadow King

## Suggested sequence

1. Spawn Cele into a small night district under sodium light.
2. Give the player a single delivery objective using honour language rather than app-economy language.
3. Route the player through a compressed urban path:
   - estate edge
   - bus stop or tram crossing
   - alley or service lane
   - takeaway or lit storefront
   - civic or institutional frontage
4. End near an institutional building where effort does not convert into heroic payoff.
5. Use that refusal to set up later contact with the Sword of Denunciation and the Shadow King.

## Environment priorities

Build a tiny place with strong spatial identity:

- one memorable junction
- one stretch of wet road
- one lit threshold
- one dead zone
- one surveillance or watcher motif

If the space feels filmable and emotionally legible, it is large enough.

## System priorities

Add only the systems the slice immediately needs:

- player spawn and traversal
- one delivery or vow prompt
- one clear route or destination signal
- one short piece of authored UI copy
- one endpoint response

Avoid early expansion into:

- combat trees
- large inventories
- broad quest frameworks
- sprawling lore databases
- city-scale simulation

## Copy direction

Use language that frames labour as ritual obligation:

- oath
- vow
- bearing
- charge
- route
- witness

Avoid making the copy purely ironic. Cele's dignity has to be emotionally real even when his worldview is unstable.
