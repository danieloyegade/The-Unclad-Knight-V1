# Creative Direction

## World

The Unclad Knight is a condensed symbolic Manchester rather than a literal map of Manchester. The city should be built from a selective grammar of spaces:

- estates
- bus stops
- tram crossings
- alleyways
- takeaways
- civic and institutional buildings
- side streets
- liminal dead zones
- wet roads
- streetlights treated as stage lights
- CCTV treated as quasi-mythic watchers

The aim is a small playable mythic GTA-like fragment, not an expansive open world.

## Tone

The target feeling is:

- urban
- nocturnal
- low-poly
- mythic
- restrained
- cinematic
- lonely
- slightly surreal
- authored rather than generic

Documentary realism should collide with theatrical stage logic. Darkness is positive space. Warm artificial light should do more of the emotional work than geometry density.

## Narrative logic

The player is Cele, a delivery rider who believes himself to be a knight.

That contradiction is foundational:

- he is noble
- he is deluded

The game should not collapse into:

- pure satire
- pure sentimentality
- straightforward heroic fantasy
- cold sociological critique with no emotional investment

Core themes:

- labour reframed as oath
- techno-feudalism
- dignity under app-mediated work
- delusion as a survival mechanism
- denunciation rather than conquest

Deliveries should feel like vows or quests. The app should feel like an invisible lord. The player should receive psychological reward through honour language, not only money language.

## Symbolic endpoint

The MVP's major endpoint is not a conventional victory state. It should move toward:

- rejection or frustration near the Arts Council building
- discovery of the Sword of Denunciation
- manifestation of the Shadow King
- recognition that the system cannot be defeated through direct violence

The Shadow King must never read as a beatable fantasy boss. Infinite health is a philosophical statement, not just a mechanical gimmick.

## Art direction

Visual strategy:

- low-detail environment
- high-impact lighting
- selective realism for key symbolic objects
- strong silhouettes
- pools of warm artificial light
- darkness used positively
- reflective pavement
- authored stage-like composition
- small, dense, memorable spaces

The game should be screen-recordable and cinematically usable. It is a playable fragment and a virtual filming location.

## Photography as source material

Photography is source material, not just moodboard reference. Real places should be transformed into low-poly playable space through authored compression:

- preserve mood
- preserve key spatial cues
- simplify geometry
- exaggerate symbolic features
- keep the space legible and playable

## Technical posture

The current project should stay:

- modular
- scalable
- Rojo-friendly
- clean
- not overengineered

When the design is unclear, add neutral scaffolding instead of forcing a large system.
